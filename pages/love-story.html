Our Love Story

The journey that brought us together

The First Meeting

Spring 2018

We first met at a mutual friend's graduation party. It was a casual introduction, but there was an instant connection that we both felt. We spent the entire evening talking and realized we had so much in common.

First Date

Summer 2018

Our first official date was at a small café downtown. We talked for hours over coffee, completely losing track of time. That's when we knew there was something special between us.

Becoming Official

Autumn 2018

After months of getting to know each other, we made it official. We celebrated with a picnic in the park, and it was one of the happiest days of our lives.

The Proposal

Winter 2022

On a snowy evening during our annual winter getaway, Rohan got down on one knee at our favorite spot overlooking the mountains. Of course, I said yes through tears of joy!

Planning Our Future

2023-2026

We spent the last year planning not just our wedding, but our life together. From choosing our first home to dreaming about future adventures, every moment has been filled with love and excitement.
